How we know we're not just guessing

A prediction is only useful if it is calibrated: when we say 70%, roughly seven of every ten of those moves should happen. The curve on the right plots what we said against what happened across 1,340 completed transfers since 2019.

We over-predict in the 30–50% band. Deals that stall in that range tend to die quietly rather than resolve, and the model still reads the noise around them as momentum. Of last window's twenty highest-rated moves, sixteen completed against nine for a naive baseline that simply follows contract length.

The misses: we had Gibbs-White at 88% to Tottenham and he stayed; Branthwaite at 24% two weeks before he left. Both were failures of the same kind — we weight reported fees heavily, and both stories were being briefed by agents rather than clubs.
